Help & info

How to order

1. Browse Products and find the item you need.
2. Tap "Place Order" on the product, or use the general order form.
3. Share your name, phone, quantity and address/pickup preference.
4. We'll call or WhatsApp you to confirm price, stock and payment.
5. Pay using one of our payment methods, or cash on delivery/pickup.

Good to know

Prices shown on this site are a guide — always confirm the final price and availability with us before paying, as medicine prices can change. For anything urgent, calling is fastest. We're open 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM, Monday to Sunday.
